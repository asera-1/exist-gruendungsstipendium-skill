# Evaluator-style review

Refresh the official handbook and any current evaluation sheet before final review. The rubric below reflects the official criteria found during the 2026-08-29 baseline review.

## Official assessment frame

During the 2026-08-29 baseline check, the official ESF project-selection framework assigned **18 points** across four categories: team (5), innovation (5), sustainability (3), and market/competition (5). The detailed EXIST handbook used a qualitative evaluator sheet and recommendation outcomes rather than publishing subcriterion point weights.

| Official ESF category | Points | Officially named topics |
|---|---:|---|
| Team | 5 | diversity, participation of women, scientific and business competence, venture, network |
| Innovation | 5 | differentiators, customer benefit, product/service description, work plan |
| Sustainability | 3 | contributions to Federal Government sustainability goals, particularly SDG 9.1a and SDG 8 |
| Market and competition | 5 | market and market development, competition, business model, financial planning, pilot customers |

The handbook's qualitative sheet places diversity and women's participation visually under Nachhaltigkeit, while the ESF point framework places them under Team. Review the facts in both relevant narratives, but count the issue once under the current official scoring framework. Never invent a diversity or SDG subscore.

Use the current official framework when available. Do not invent a pass threshold, approval probability, or unofficial subcriterion weights. The traffic-light diagnostic below is for revision priority only and must never be presented as an official score.

## Diagnostic scale

This is an internal readiness scale, not an official score:

- **Green:** specific, credible, consistent, and supported.
- **Yellow:** present but weak, ambiguous, generic, or incompletely evidenced.
- **Red:** missing, contradicted, apparently ineligible, or materially unsupported.

## Pass 1: formal gate

Check:

- current official Ideenpapier template, permitted language, 15-page/format rule, and the source authorizing each attachment; the outline permits LOIs, while the handbook additionally named mockups and video links and the live page used broader wording;
- current application window; expected receipt of a complete application; the binding minimum of three months from complete receipt to project start at the 2026 baseline; institution-specific lead time; and the fixed 12-month standard duration, with only the narrow currently permitted childbirth/parental-support extension treated separately;
- complete and internally consistent application artifacts: current EXIST-generator PDF/XML, correct AZA/AZV in easy-Online, signed PDFs and declarations, current signature method, email step, and local checklist;
- unresolved 2026 submission conflict: the handbook described emailing XML and signed AZA/AZV to an `@ptj.de` destination, while the live page/generator described emailing the XML to an `@fz-juelich.de` destination; require a current written institutional/PtJ answer before `submission-ready`;
- paperless filing under the live operational instructions, while retaining signed originals as currently required; do not revive postal filing from the older guideline without current confirmation;
- applicant institution and qualifying network;
- one named mentor in the official category Hochschullehrer/in or Arbeitsgruppenleiter/in who is employed by or on staff at the applicant institution, acts as project lead, and provides the current signed declaration;
- institutional readiness to conclude the current stipend agreement with each proposed funded person;
- one to three stipend holders, at least one university graduate, each person's project-start category and exact qualification/employment dates, student progress and study format, availability, know-how contribution, and the single vocational/over-five-year exception limit;
- solo-student, solo-vocational, solo-over-five-year and majority-student cases against all current official sources, with the official conflict escalated instead of assumed away;
- prior EXIST, successful EXIST-Women, and comparable state innovation-startup funding, recording program, start/end, duration, sequence and venture overlap; apply content/time separation to repeat EXIST only, and verify the current program-specific sequencing and 12-month pre-EGS limit for comparable state funding;
- for a non-EU person, institution-verified residence-title coverage for the full requested period at project start; for a qualification from a non-EU state, institution-retained recognition evidence; and for another foreign qualification, any required institutional equivalency check. Record verification and evidence holder rather than collecting scans or document numbers;
- company formation and business-activity timing;
- overlapping active/dormant employment, stipends, living-cost support, and paid side work, including monthly hours and dates; escalate the binding-guideline versus handbook conflict for any active employment;
- current handling of certificates, birth records and residence evidence: verify/retain at the institution and upload only when the current process or a case-specific request requires it;
- any institution-specific internal deadline, procurement requirement, or additional local form.

Any ambiguity that could block formal review is `Critical` until confirmed.

## Pass 2: substantive criteria

### Innovation and feasibility

- Innovation compared with the state of the art is concrete and material.
- Product, technology, or service is understandable.
- IP or alternative protection strategy is addressed.
- Customer need and benefit are evident.
- R&D groundwork and feasibility evidence are plausible.
- Project/work plan is realistic in relation to the idea.

### Team and implementation capacity

- Scientific and technical competence covers the work.
- Business and entrepreneurial competence is present or credibly added.
- Industry knowledge is present or credibly added.
- Roles, workload, availability, and expected ownership are clear.
- Institution, network, mentor, advisers, and other support are relevant and credible.
- Material team gaps are acknowledged with a concrete closure plan.
- The contribution of women and other relevant diversity dimensions is factual, role-specific, and consistent with the composition and ownership plan; sensitive personal facts are not solicited merely to decorate the narrative.

### Entrepreneurial implementation

- Direct, indirect, and status-quo alternatives are analyzed.
- Advantages are relevant, evidenced, and defensible.
- Initial market-entry segment is precise and plausible.
- Partners and multipliers are distinguished as confirmed or prospective.
- Customer discovery, pilot users, or LOIs are accurately represented.
- Market opportunities and entry barriers are explicit.
- Pricing is grounded and competitive.
- Sales plan, P&L, break-even logic, and financing concept reconcile.

### Sustainability

- Contribution to relevant sustainability objectives is causal, measurable where possible, and not generic.
- SDG 9.1a and SDG 8, which the ESF framework particularly names, are addressed where relevant; any different or additional SDG is tied to a specific target/indicator and credible mechanism rather than a logo list.
- Baseline, system boundary, comparison case, expected qualitative/quantitative change, measurement method, attribution, material adverse effects, and mitigation reconcile.
- Any cross-reference to diversity/women aligns with the team and ownership information and is not double-counted as a second demographic claim.

### Mittelplanung and application deliverability

Do not add an unofficial fifth score category. Use this as a feasibility and compliance check across the official criteria:

- current baseline ceilings are correctly separated: EUR 5,000 Coachingausgaben; EUR 30,000 Sachausgaben for a team or EUR 10,000 for a solo founder;
- every item maps to a work package, milestone, timing, evidence basis, eligible recipient, and the institution's procurement route;
- coaching stays within the EUR 1,000 net day rate including travel and is strategic/gruendungsbezogen; technical/design work is treated as Sachausgaben;
- routine company expenses, direct formation costs, company-addressed invoices, and costs outside the effective project period are excluded;
- invoices normally name the applicant/grant-recipient institution, with any permitted private-address exception documented;
- outside-EU suppliers and all external procurement are cleared under the current official/institutional rules, including the procurement changes effective from 2026-07-01;
- a later adjustment to the plan is not treated as permission to ignore caps, category, invoice, procurement, project-period, or company-separation rules.

## Pass 3: evidence and consistency

Search across every document for:

- different project names or acronyms;
- conflicting founder names, roles, qualifications, dates, workloads, or share allocations;
- different company or activity status descriptions;
- customer counts or quotes without source records;
- market-size figures with different definitions or years;
- technical performance claims without test conditions or evidence;
- milestones that do not produce the capabilities promised elsewhere;
- expenses that do not map to work packages;
- different project-start classifications, qualification dates, residence-title coverage periods, recognition status, or evidence holders between the intake and forms;
- a solo/team or student/exception constellation treated as settled despite a recorded official-source conflict;
- prior EXIST/EXIST-Women/comparable funding omitted from one application artifact;
- an active employment case reduced to only a monthly-hours number without the binding-rule conflict being resolved;
- attachment, language, duration, signature, XML/AZA/AZV, email-destination, or upload claims that do not cite the current source/local answer;
- sales, price, volume, revenue, cost, cash, and break-even inconsistencies;
- partners, advisers, pilots, IP rights, or LOIs described as confirmed when they are only intended;
- present tense used for planned features or evidence;
- unsupported legal, tax, regulatory, or eligibility conclusions.

Also reconcile these shared ledgers across sections:

- current capability, funded target, and market-entry threshold;
- user, buyer, payer, gatekeeper, and initial segment;
- confirmed, conditional, prospective, and unverified partner status;
- founder qualification, work-package ownership, availability, and future role;
- price, volume, conversion, sales cycle, revenue, cost, cash need, financing, and break-even;
- work package, milestone, expense, timing, and eligibility confirmation.
- the same performance baseline, system boundary, comparison period, and attribution logic across innovation, customer benefit, milestones, sustainability, and financial savings.
- the official diversity/SDG crosswalk, so women/diversity are not scored twice or displaced from the Team evidence.

## Review output

Lead with the most consequential finding.

| Severity | Section | Finding and evidence | Why it matters | Required correction | Owner/confirmation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Critical / Major / Minor |  |  |  |  |  |

Then provide:

1. readiness by rubric area;
2. unresolved formal questions;
3. unsupported-claim list;
4. cross-document consistency result;
5. prioritized revision sequence;
6. questions for the Gründungsnetzwerk or PtJ.

Do not rewrite a weak claim until its missing evidence or assumption is visible. Do not convert the diagnostic result into an approval probability.

## Nachbesserung mode

For reviewer feedback:

1. Quote or paraphrase each comment accurately.
2. Identify the underlying criterion and affected sections.
3. Separate requests for clarification, new evidence, recalculation, and structural revision.
4. Build a response matrix with owner, source evidence, proposed change, and consistency impacts.
5. Revise only after material facts are confirmed.
6. Run the full consistency pass again.
