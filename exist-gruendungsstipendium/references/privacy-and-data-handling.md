# Privacy and data handling

Read this reference whenever personal or confidential material is supplied, requested, exported, searched, or sent to another tool or recipient.

## What this package does and does not do

The Skill is a static set of Markdown instructions. It has no server, telemetry, analytics, credentials, or applicant database. The model host and any enabled browsing, connector, storage, or external tool still process data under their own current terms, plan, workspace configuration, and retention rules. Do not describe use of the Skill as local-only, anonymous, zero-retention, or GDPR-compliant unless the actual deployment has been independently verified to support that statement.

## Before using real applicant material

1. **Define the task and data boundary.** Identify the purpose, data categories, responsible organization or person, intended recipients, and only the minimum information needed.
2. **Check authority and lawfulness separately.** Permission to use confidential material does not automatically establish a GDPR lawful basis. Where personal data is involved, the responsible controller must address the lawful basis for each processing or transfer step, transparency, controller/processor roles, provider terms, security, retention and deletion, subprocessors, data-subject rights, and international transfers as applicable.
3. **Use an approved environment.** For institutional work, use the account, workspace, model, and tools approved by the institution. Verify current training or model-improvement controls, chat or task history, retention and deletion, administrator access, connectors, browsing, subprocessors, and transfer arrangements before uploading material.
4. **Minimize before upload.** Prefer synthetic placeholders or genuinely anonymous data. Remove unnecessary identifiers and sensitive attachments. Redaction is not anonymization when a person can still be identified from the project, institution, role, dates, CV details, or a combination of facts.
5. **Keep external actions separate.** Use generic official-source searches. Do not place applicant data or confidential details into a query, URL, connector, external tool, email, or upload unless that transfer is necessary, specifically permitted for confidentiality, and allowed under the responsible controller's process.
6. **Review before sharing.** Check clean drafts, filenames, metadata, comments, exports, and attachments for personal data, confidential facts, provenance, unsupported claims, and unintended recipients.

If any check is unresolved, continue with placeholders and record the question for the responsible privacy or data-protection contact. Do not request identity documents merely to draft narrative sections.
