# Ideenpapier drafting guide

Always obtain and use the current official outline. The structure below reflects the official outline found during the 2026-08-29 baseline review; verify it before final drafting.

## Current baseline structure

The baseline outline limits the Ideenpapier to 15 pages using Arial 12 pt and 1.5 line spacing. It expressly permits LOIs as additional material. The 2026 handbook also names mockups and links to videos, while the live application page refers more broadly to supporting documents. These official descriptions are not identical. Do not assume that a cover, contents page, financial appendix, survey appendix, or another self-created attachment sits outside the limit. Count it unless the current official material or the responsible Gründungsnetzwerk/PtJ explicitly confirms otherwise. Record which source authorizes each attachment.

At the 2026 baseline, an English Ideenpapier was an exception for a team with no German-speaking member and required current confirmation. Do not infer that the remaining application may be submitted in English. Confirm the permitted language before drafting and keep the current institutional/PtJ answer in the compliance record.

### 1. Geschäftsidee — approximately 6 pages

#### 1.1 Innovation — approximately 3 pages

Cover:

- the technology/product innovation or knowledge-based innovative service;
- how it works at the level required to judge novelty and feasibility;
- state of the art, direct and indirect alternatives, and measurable differentiation;
- present evidence from R&D, prototypes, experiments, or prior work;
- protection strategy: patents or other rights where relevant, secrecy, lead time, data, know-how, contracts, or another reasoned approach.

Avoid unsupported superlatives. Separate demonstrated capabilities from planned development.

#### 1.2 Kundennutzen — approximately 0.5 page

State:

- precise initial target group and buyer/user distinction;
- painful problem and current workaround;
- concrete benefit, preferably measurable;
- evidence from interviews, tests, pilots, or other validation.

The short space demands a crisp problem–customer–benefit statement, not broad market prose.

#### 1.3 Gründungsvorgeschichte — approximately 0.5 page

Explain:

- who originated the idea;
- prior research, study, employment, or projects that produced the know-how;
- the relationship to the applicant institution;
- relevant ownership, permissions, and contributions.

Do not exaggerate an academic link or omit contributors and rights holders.

#### 1.4 Stand der Umsetzung — approximately 1 page

Create three explicit states:

1. capabilities and evidence that exist now;
2. features and validation to be completed during the grant;
3. further work needed for the first marketable version after or beyond the funded work.

Include readiness evidence and unresolved technical, regulatory, data, supply, or adoption risks.

#### 1.5 Projektplanung — approximately 1 page

Map the funded period into work packages, owners, outputs, milestones, evidence of completion, dependencies, and risk responses. The 2026 handbook described the standard project period as exactly 12 months; build a 12-month plan unless a current official case-specific rule says otherwise. A narrow one-time extension of up to three months existed for the specified childbirth/parental-support case, but it is not ordinary project capacity and must not be pre-booked as extra delivery time. Connect the final milestone to market entry or follow-on financing.

Use a compact table when helpful:

| Period | Work package | Owner | Deliverable | Success evidence | Dependency/risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Milestones must be outcomes that can be verified, not activities such as “work on marketing.”

### 2. Team — approximately 3 pages

Show:

- project-relevant qualifications, research, professional, entrepreneurial, and industry experience;
- relevant contributions from non-funded team members as well as the proposed stipend holders;
- why each person can deliver assigned work packages;
- planned roles, decision responsibilities, company structure, and expected share allocation;
- mentor, scientific/business advisers, partners, and industry contacts;
- missing competencies or resources and a credible plan, owner, and timing to close each gap;
- factual contribution of women and other relevant diversity dimensions where the current outline requests it.

Do not paste CVs. Build a role–evidence–work-package argument. Do not invent future hires, advisers, ownership, or commitments.

### 3. Unternehmerische Umsetzung — approximately 5 pages

#### 3.1 Wettbewerb — approximately 2 pages

Compare direct competitors, indirect alternatives, internal solutions, and doing nothing. Use criteria important to the target customer and cite reliable sources in the working draft.

| Alternative | Target user | Relevant capability | Price/cost where evidenced | Limitation | Defensible advantage |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Never write “no competitors.” If the category is new, compare against the current workaround and substitute solutions.

#### 3.2 Markteintritt — approximately 1 page

Cover:

- narrow entry segment and why it comes first;
- buyer, user, sales path, channel, and expected sales cycle;
- implementation and market-entry partners, distinguishing confirmed from desired;
- customer conversations, sample, findings, changes made, pilots, and LOIs;
- required approvals, certifications, standards, reimbursement, procurement, or other barriers;
- first repeatable proof that would validate entry.

Do not turn intended outreach into completed validation.

#### 3.3 Finanzplanung — approximately 1.5 pages

Include:

- business and pricing model, price basis, and competitor comparison;
- sales-unit assumptions and simple profit-and-loss logic through break-even;
- major cost drivers, gross margin where relevant, hiring or operating assumptions;
- financing requirements, timing, sources, and credible next milestone;
- base case and the most decision-relevant sensitivity.

Every important number must trace to a calculation, source, quote, experiment, or explicitly labelled founder assumption. Mathematical consistency does not make an assumption true.

#### 3.4 Nachhaltigkeit — approximately 0.5 page

Select only relevant sustainability goals. Explain the causal mechanism, baseline, expected qualitative and quantitative contribution, measurement method, and material negative trade-offs or mitigation. Avoid listing SDGs without operational relevance.

Use this official crosswalk rather than treating every sustainability topic as interchangeable:

- The ESF selection framework assigns 3 of 18 points to contributions to the Federal Government's sustainability goals and particularly names SDG 9.1a and SDG 8. Address those two where they genuinely fit, or explain why another relevant contribution is the stronger causal match; do not fabricate a link.
- The official outline places women and other team-diversity information in the Team section. The 2026 handbook's qualitative sheet displays diversity/women under Nachhaltigkeit, while the ESF point framework counts them in Team. Put the factual team description in Section 2 and cross-reference it here only when it has a genuine sustainability implication. Do not double-count or pad the section with demographic labels.
- For any additional SDG, identify the applicable target or indicator, the mechanism created by this venture, the baseline and measurement boundary. A logo list is not evidence.

### 4. Mittelplanung für die Vorhabenlaufzeit — approximately 0.5 page

Use the official terms **Coachingausgaben** and **Sachausgaben** and refresh the current expenditure and procurement sources before calculating. At the 2026-08-29 baseline, the outline allowed EUR 5,000 for Coachingausgaben and EUR 30,000 for Sachausgaben for a team, reduced to EUR 10,000 for a solo founder. These are ceilings/categories, not a promise that every planned item is eligible.

Separate the two categories. For each line connect purpose, timing, estimate basis, funded milestone, intended invoice recipient, and the institution's eligibility/procurement confirmation.

| Category | Purpose | Timing | Estimate basis | Milestone enabled | Invoice/procurement route | Confirmation needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Apply these baseline classification rules, then have the institution confirm the actual plan:

- A coach's day rate was capped at EUR 1,000 net including travel. Coaching can cover external strategic advice and preparation of the commercial or tax structure of the future venture. Routine company tax work, accounting, bank fees, and other company operating costs are not coaching expenses.
- Technical or design consulting and training belong under Sachausgaben, not Coachingausgaben. Project-related third-party development may also be a Sachausgabe, but the funded institution—not the founders—must procure and contract it under the applicable rules.
- Direct formation costs such as notary, registration, trade-registration, or company-tax-return costs are ineligible. Do not disguise them as coaching or legal strategy.
- Under the still-listed 2023 expense guide, invoices normally name the grant-recipient institution; a founder's private address is only a reasoned exception. An invoice to a partnership or capital company is ineligible. Costs must arise and become effective within the project period, with time-based services apportioned where necessary.
- The 2023 guide required PtJ coordination for a provider outside the EU and limited that route to cases with no EU provider or an offer at least 50% cheaper. Because that guide is dated, recheck the condition before vendor selection.
- Do not use the 2023 guide's procurement wording as the only current rule. Official information effective from 2026-07-01 describes new procurement relief, including a EUR 15,000 net direct-award threshold where the stated federal conditions apply, plus separate negotiated-procedure and startup-supplier rules. The institution's procurement office must determine which rule applies; founders must not self-award or split orders to avoid a procedure.

The outline says the plan may be adjusted during the funding period. That flexibility does not waive category limits, project-period effectiveness, invoice-recipient, procurement, or separation-from-company requirements. Never assume an item is allowable merely because it fits under a current cap. Flag taxes, procurement, travel, equipment, external services, IP, software, third-party development, and non-EU suppliers for institutional review where necessary.

## Section workflow

Use this workflow internally; do not force the founder through a visible preamble or questionnaire when the supplied evidence already supports a useful draft:

1. Identify the evaluator question and applicable current outline points.
2. Separate supported facts from estimates, hypotheses, inferences, contradictions, and missing evidence.
3. Ask only questions that block an accurate draft, no more than five at a time. Otherwise draft immediately.
4. Produce clean founder-facing prose first when support is sufficient. Use visible `[MISSING: …]` markers only in a clearly labelled working draft.
5. Keep a short evidence/gap note separate from the application prose.
6. Check the result against the relevant reviewer criteria and reconcile all repeated numbers and claims with other sections.

Page allocations are layout constraints, not exact word counts. Leave room for tables, figures, captions, and revision. Attachments strengthen evidence but should not replace an explanation required in the Ideenpapier. Never imitate a previous application's page count, font size, spacing, attachment list, or form package; run a fresh compliance check against the current official material.

## Writing quality

- Start paragraphs with the conclusion, then evidence and implication.
- Define specialized terms and acronyms on first use.
- Prefer quantified comparison and specific examples over “innovative,” “unique,” or “huge market.”
- Make the links explicit: customer problem → innovation → benefit → evidence → milestone → business model.
- Keep hypotheses honest. A credible validation plan is stronger than fabricated certainty.
- Preserve the founder’s technical meaning and voice.
